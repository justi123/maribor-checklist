✅ Important Documents (Carry Originals + Copies)

Keep these ready before visiting offices.

Passport (Original)Must carry every time you go to official offices.
Visa + Entry StampTake a copy of the visa page and entry stamp page.
Rent Agreement / Accommodation ProofNeeded for address registration and residence permit.
Enrollment Certificate (Potrdilo o vpisu)You will get it from the university after enrollment.
Health Insurance ProofRequired for residence permit approval.
Proof of FundsBank statement / scholarship / sponsor proof (as required).
✅ Tip: Make at least 2 photocopies of each document and keep phone scans.

✅ 3-Day Plan (Easy & Realistic)

DAY 1 — Setup & Registration
Address registration + University documents
Most urgent
Register Temporary AddressUpravna Enota (Foreigners office). Bring passport + visa + rent agreement.
University Enrollment / Student ConfirmationUniversity International Office. Request enrollment certificate.
Buy / Recharge Bus Card (Optional)Useful for daily travel. Add your Bus Card location on the right to get routes.
Train Discount / Travel Option (Optional)Check student discounts if you travel between cities.
DAY 2 — Residence Permit
Biometrics + permit submission
Main step
Submit Residence Permit + BiometricsReturn to Upravna Enota with address paper + enrollment certificate + insurance + proof of funds.
DAY 3 — Support Steps
Tax number + insurance + work registration
Support steps
Apply for Tax Number (Davčna številka)FURS office. Needed for jobs + salary + bank account.
Health Insurance (If pending)ZZZS office. Take passport + address registration.
Open Bank Account (Optional)Choose any bank. Take passport + tax number.
Register in Študentski servis (Student Work Service)After you get tax number + student confirmation, register for student work.
